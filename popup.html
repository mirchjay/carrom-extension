Carrom Rules & Guide

  • Objective: Clear all pieces from the board to achieve the highest possible score.
  • White Coin: 10 Points
  • Black Coin: 5 Points
  • Queen (Red Coin): 50 Points
  • Foul Penalty: If the striker falls into a pocket, you lose 10 Points.
  • Aim & Power: Click and drag backward from the striker to aim and charge power. Release to shoot.
  • Reposition: Use the slider below the board to move your striker along the baseline before shooting.
