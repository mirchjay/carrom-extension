Carrom Rules & Guide

  • 1P vs Computer: Play against an AI opponent. You are White (10 pts), Computer is Black (10 pts).
  • Pass & Play (2P): Local 2-Player mode. Player 1 (White) vs Player 2 (Black).
  • Queen (Red): Worth 50 Points to whoever pockets it.
  • Turn Rules: Pocketing your assigned color grants another turn. Missing or pocketing an opponent piece switches turns.
  • Foul Penalty: Pocketing striker deducts 10 Points and ends turn.
