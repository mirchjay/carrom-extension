Carrom Rules & Guide

  • Singleplayer: Pocket all pieces for high score.
  • Pass & Play (2P): Player 1 (White - 10 pts) vs Player 2 (Black - 5 pts).
  • Queen (Red): Worth 50 Points to whoever pockets it.
  • Turn Rules (2P): Pocketing your own color gives you another turn. Missing or pocketing opponent's coin switches turn.
  • Foul Penalty: Pocketing striker loses 10 Points and ends turn.
  • Aiming: Drag backward from the striker to set direction and power.
